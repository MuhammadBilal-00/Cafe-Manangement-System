1Executive Summary

The Cafe Management System is a single platform that runs an entire food business — from the moment a cashier rings up an order, through the kitchen, the stock room, the accounts ledger, and the payroll run at the end of the month.

Most restaurants juggle separate tools: one app for billing, spreadsheets for stock, a diary for attendance, and an accountant who re-enters everything at month-end. Every hand-off loses time and introduces errors. This system replaces all of that with one connected platform where a sale at the register automatically updates orders, the kitchen screen, stock levels, the general ledger, and your sales reports — with no re-typing and no reconciliation gaps.

One system, zero re-entry
A POS sale drives the kitchen ticket, deducts recipe ingredients from stock, posts to the accounting ledger, and appears in reports — automatically.
Built for multiple branches
Run every location from one dashboard: per-branch menus, stock transfers between branches, branch-level reports and targets.
Enterprise-grade navigation
Modules are organized the way your team actually works — cashier, kitchen, accountant, HR — with instant search (Ctrl+K) to jump anywhere in two keystrokes.
Trustworthy numbers
Revenue is recognized from real invoices, every stock movement is traceable, and a full audit log records who did what, when.

4A Premium, Modern Experience

  • ERP-grade navigation — modules grouped by business domain in a collapsible sidebar that remembers how each user likes it; a first-time manager finds anything in seconds.
  • Instant search (Ctrl+K) — a command palette that jumps to any module the user is allowed to see, in two keystrokes.
  • Keyboard-first operations — “P” opens the register from anywhere, “N” starts a new record, “/” jumps to search.
  • Real-time everywhere — kitchen screens, order boards and notifications update live, without refreshing.
  • English and Urdu (اردو) — full bilingual interface with proper right-to-left layout; switch languages in one click.
  • Light and dark themes — comfortable at the morning till and the midnight close alike.
  • Works on any device — responsive layout for desktops at the counter, tablets in the kitchen, phones on the floor.

5Security & Reliability

  • Role-based permissions enforced on every action on the server — not just hidden buttons.
  • Complete audit trail — logins, edits, payroll runs, cancellations and administrative changes are recorded with user, time and detail.
  • Strict data isolation — built as a multi-tenant platform with automated tests proving one business can never see another's data.
  • Honest accounting — cancellations reverse their ledger entries; revenue reports are built from issued invoices; payroll refuses invalid runs.
  • Proven technology — Microsoft ASP.NET Core and SQL Server: mature, supported, and widely trusted in enterprise environments.
